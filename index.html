// ABOUT US

Technology For The Future And Sustainable Society

JZUMING IT SERVICES is a reputable provider of IT, electrical, and air conditioning solutions headquartered in Kota Bharu, Kelantan. Established in 2016, the company began as a dedicated computer repair service before expanding to meet the evolving technological demands of its clients.

Today, the company delivers a comprehensive portfolio of services, including computer sales, networking solutions, CCTV systems, electrical installations, and air conditioning services. Guided by a commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction, JZUMING IT SERVICES continues to serve both individuals and organizations with professionalism and reliability.
